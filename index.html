The control center now lives inside SignalWall.

Open it from the tray. Reorder screens, tune motion, create themes, and import or export complete presets from one place.

  • Global or per-screen quote and color themes
  • Five seconds to five minutes per quote
  • Light and dark control-center themes
  • Shareable preset files for complete setups
